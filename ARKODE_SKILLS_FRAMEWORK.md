# Arkode Skills Framework
**A Guide to Standardizing Business Operations with Claude Skills**

---

## Business Areas to Consider

### 1. Client Communication
**Purpose:** Standardize how Arkode communicates with clients

**Potential Skills:**
- `arkode-client-onboarding` - Welcome emails, kickoff materials
- `arkode-proposal-writer` - Service proposals, SOWs
- `arkode-status-updates` - Weekly/monthly client updates
- `arkode-meeting-notes` - Client meeting summaries
- `arkode-invoice-emails` - Professional invoice communications

**Key Information Needed:**
- Tone/voice guidelines
- Email templates
- Service offerings
- Pricing structure
- Brand guidelines

## Skill Priority Matrix

### Start Here (High Impact, Low Effort)
1. **Email/Communication Templates** - Immediate consistency
2. **Meeting Notes Format** - Capture decisions systematically
3. **Status Update Template** - Regular client communication

### Quick Wins (High Impact, Medium Effort)
4. **Proposal Generator** - Faster sales cycle
5. **Project Estimator** - Accurate scoping
6. **Service Catalog** - Clear offerings

### Strategic Builds (High Impact, High Effort)
7. **Client Onboarding System** - Complete workflow
8. **Knowledge Base Builder** - Institutional memory
9. **Code Review Guidelines** - Quality assurance

## Skill Standardization Checklist

For each Arkode skill, ensure you have:

### Core Requirements
- [ ] Clear trigger conditions (when to use this skill)
- [ ] Step-by-step workflow
- [ ] Examples of good outputs
- [ ] Tone/style guidelines
- [ ] Success criteria

### Brand Consistency
- [ ] Arkode logo and visual assets
- [ ] Color palette (if applicable)
- [ ] Typography standards
- [ ] Email/document signatures
- [ ] Legal disclaimers (if needed)

### Domain Knowledge
- [ ] Company-specific terminology
- [ ] Service descriptions
- [ ] Pricing guidelines
- [ ] Technology preferences
- [ ] Client industries served

### Quality Control
- [ ] Required information to gather
- [ ] Validation checks
- [ ] Error handling
- [ ] Approval workflows
- [ ] Revision history

## Getting Started: Your First 3 Skills

### Skill #1: Arkode Client Email
**Complexity:** Simple (15 minutes)
**Impact:** Immediate brand consistency
**What you'll need:**
- Email signature format
- Common email scenarios (intro, update, invoice, etc.)
- Tone guidelines (professional, friendly, technical)

### Skill #2: Arkode Status Updates
**Complexity:** Simple (20 minutes)
**Impact:** Better client communication
**What you'll need:**
- Update frequency (weekly/monthly)
- Standard sections (progress, plans, blockers)
- Formatting preferences

### Skill #3: Arkode Service Proposal
**Complexity:** Medium (45 minutes)
**Impact:** Faster sales cycle
**What you'll need:**
- Service offerings list
- Pricing tiers
- Proposal template structure
- Past successful proposals (examples)
- Legal terms/disclaimers

## Skill Naming Convention for Arkode

**Format:** `arkode-[category]-[function]`

**Examples:**
- `arkode-client-proposal`
- `arkode-project-estimate`
- `arkode-team-standup`
- `arkode-tech-architecture`

**Benefits:**
- Easy to identify Arkode skills
- Clear categorization
- Searchable naming
- Professional appearance

## Next Steps

1. **Audit Current Workflows** - Identify repetitive tasks
2. **Prioritize Skills** - Start with high-impact, low-effort
3. **Gather Resources** - Collect templates, examples, guidelines
4. **Build Iteratively** - Create, test, improve
5. **Document Everything** - Make skills the source of truth

---

## Questions to Answer for Each Skill

1. **When should this skill be used?** (Trigger conditions)
2. **What does success look like?** (Output quality)
3. **What information is needed?** (Inputs)
4. **What's the workflow?** (Step-by-step process)
5. **What can go wrong?** (Edge cases, errors)
6. **How often will this be used?** (ROI calculation)
